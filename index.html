Part 1

See, Think, Wonder

Write one paragraph under each heading. Your drafts save in this browser as you type.

I see…

Factual observations only. What is actually there?

  • Subject(s), figures, objects
  • Medium and materials
  • Color, line, texture, condition

I think…

Logical inferences, grounded in this week's reading.

  • Possible themes or meaning
  • Likely time period
  • Culture or place of origin

I wonder…

Open-ended questions for your classmates.

  • What is still unclear?
  • What would you ask the maker?
  • Avoid yes/no questions
